SpiralGalaxy

This is an interactive particle scene built with three.js and WebGL. Every glowing point is one of tens of thousands of particles, moved on the GPU by a custom shader. As you scroll, the same particles travel from a spiral galaxy to a scattered cloud, then a triangle, and out to the edges of the screen. Move your cursor across them to drag them along and watch them settle back into place.
